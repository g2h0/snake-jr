# Snake Jr. 🐍

[▶ Play Snake Jr.](https://g2h0.xyz/snake-jr/)

A fast, colorful snake game made for quick, kid-friendly fun. Swipe to turn, gobble apples, chase the legendary **✨ 67 ✨ apple**, unlock new snake skins, and post your score to the global leaderboard.

## How to play

| | |
|---|---|
| Move | Swipe anywhere on the play area. On desktop, use the arrow keys or WASD. |
| Walls | Wrap around — no death on edges. |
| Stay alive | Running into your own tail ends the game. |
| Speed | Increases with every apple eaten. |
| 🍎 | +1 point. |
| ✨ 67 ✨ | Rare golden apple, +67 points. Disappears after 8 seconds. |
| Combo | Eat the next regular apple quickly to build a combo up to 5× (MAX AURA). Golden apples are always exactly +67. |
| Milestones | Hit special scores to trigger celebration banners. |
| Progress | Your personal best fills the meter toward the next snake or world. |
| Worlds | Unlock and choose new playfield themes as your personal best grows. |
| Pause / Mute | ⏸ and 🔊 live in the top-right HUD. Auto-pauses if the app is backgrounded. |

## Tips

- Use the wraparound walls to escape tight spots.
- Chain regular apples quickly for bigger combo points.
- Plan ahead as the snake gets longer and faster.
- Golden apples vanish quickly, but they are worth **67 points**.

After a run, choose an emoji and enter a three-letter tag to put your score on the global leaderboard.
