# Progression ideas — parked

These ideas came out of the July 2026 child play sessions. They are intentionally
not part of the current build, but are saved here for possible future playtests.

## One mission per run

Offer one short, skill-appropriate challenge such as eating five apples, surviving
30 seconds, wrapping through three walls, reaching a 3× combo, or beating the
personal best by one point. Avoid making the random golden apple a requirement.

## Snake Book achievements

Create a permanent badge page with playful accomplishments such as First Bite,
Wall Wizard, Combo Kid, Super Long Snake, Golden Hunter, 67 Club, Comeback Kid,
and MAX AURA.

## Collectible cosmetics

Completing a small set of missions could hatch a mystery egg containing a hat,
trail, apple style, confetti palette, or funny game-over effect. Keep rewards
simple and legible rather than introducing a currency or shop.

## Product guardrails

- No daily streaks or penalties for taking a break.
- No energy timers, expiring rewards, ads, or paid loot boxes.
- Give progress for play and learning, not repetitive grinding.
- Add one system at a time and validate it in child play sessions.
